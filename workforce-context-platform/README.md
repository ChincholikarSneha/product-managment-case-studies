#  Workforce Context Platform

## Overview

Workforce Context Platform is a product strategy case study that reimagines corporate learning by shifting the focus from **skill acquisition** to **business understanding**. Instead of simply teaching employees new skills, the platform helps them understand organizational context, customer journeys, business models, and cross-functional workflows to enable faster and more meaningful contributions. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

---

##  Problem Statement

While AI has made learning technical skills easier, many employees still struggle to understand **how their work contributes to the business**. Organizations invest heavily in training, yet new hires often take months to become effective because they lack business context rather than technical knowledge. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

---

##  Proposed Solution

The Workforce Context Platform introduces a learning experience that helps employees understand:

- Customer journeys
- Business models
- Product ecosystem
- Organizational workflows
- Context-based simulations
- Organizational intelligence for managers

The long-term vision is to reduce time-to-contribution by making organizational knowledge accessible and actionable. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}
