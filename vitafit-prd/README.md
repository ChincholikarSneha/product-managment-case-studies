# VitaFit Momentum
### Building Fitness Consistency Through Coaching and Community

**North Star Metric: Day-30 Retention Rate. Proposed improvement target: +20%.**

> Product requirements case study with a wireframe, not a working prototype. All improvement figures are proposed targets.

## 1. Executive Summary

### The Pitch
VitaFit Momentum helps beginner and intermediate home-workout users build consistent routines through personalized guidance and small fitness communities. It combines an Adaptive Coaching Journey with instructor-led Fitness Circles to support motivation and accountability.

### My Role
Problem framing, research synthesis, audience definition, feature requirements, MVP scoping, metric selection, experiment planning, and wireframe design.

### Headline Metric
**Target: Improve Day-30 retention by 20%.**

## 2. Problem Statement & Market Context

### The Pain Point
Users begin with high motivation but gradually disengage because of:
- Repetitive workout discovery.
- Generic reminders.
- Limited accountability.
- Weak connection with instructors.
- Plans that do not adapt to changing needs.

### Business Opportunity
Improve retention and repeat usage by helping users follow a manageable routine and receive timely coaching and community support.

### Target Personas
- **Primary:** Working professionals aged 22–40 who are beginner or intermediate home-workout users.
- **Secondary:** Women seeking adaptive menstrual or pregnancy workout plans.

## 3. Product Strategy & Frameworks

### Research & Insights
Secondary research from user reviews, Reddit discussions, and fitness-app studies highlighted:
- Motivation declines after the first few weeks.
- Tracking alone does not sustain engagement.
- Generic reminders are often ignored.
- Coaching and small communities can support consistency.
- Accountability can help users maintain routines.

### Prioritisation
Used insight-to-feature mapping to connect engagement barriers with product decisions.

| User Insight | Product Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Initial motivation fades. | Personalized weekly plans and one daily target. |
| Generic reminders are ignored. | Contextual coach-style messages. |
| Users feel isolated. | Fitness Circles with up to 10 members. |
| Users need accountability. | Shared goals and instructor check-ins. |
| Workout needs change. | Adaptive plans and recovery guidance. |

### Scope / MVP Definition
The proposed MVP includes:
- Personalized onboarding.
- Weekly workout plans.
- Coach-style reminders.
- Fitness Circles.

The broader feature design also includes recovery guidance, weekly feedback, and adaptive workout experiences. Detailed release boundaries for these capabilities remain to be finalized.

## 4. Solution & Execution

### Feature 1: Adaptive Coaching Journey
- Capture goals, physical details, and schedule during onboarding.
- Generate a proposed seven-day workout plan with one daily target.
- Guide users toward stretching, breathing, or mobility after completing their goal.
- Celebrate consistency and gently re-engage inactive users.
- Refine the following week's plan through weekly feedback.

### Feature 2: Fitness Circles
- Unlock after two weeks of consistent usage.
- Match up to 10 members by goal, fitness level, and schedule.
- Offer instructor-led weekly consistency challenges.
- Support shared milestones and lightweight reactions.
- Include recovery-focused challenges for adaptive circles.

These capabilities are proposed requirements represented through the wireframe.

### Cross-functional Execution
The proposed delivery plan involves:
- **Design:** Onboarding, coaching, and community experiences.
- **Engineering:** Plan generation, reminders, matching, and progress tracking.
- **Instructors:** Challenge design and community guidance.
- **Relevant experts:** Review of adaptive workout guidance.

**Proposed timeline:** Research and wireframes in weeks 1–2, development in weeks 3–6, QA in week 7, beta in week 8, and launch in week 10.

## 5. Impact & Key Metrics

### User Metrics — Proposed Targets
- **Day-30 retention:** +20%.
- **Average weekly workouts per user:** +25%.
- **Weekly plan completion:** +30%.
- **Fitness Circle adoption:** 40%.

### Business Metrics
- Retention as the primary indicator of sustained engagement.
- No revenue or paid-conversion target is specified.

### Validation & Next Iteration
A/B test the proposed MVP against the existing experience.

Track:
- Day-30 retention.
- Weekly workout sessions.
- Challenge participation.
- Notification engagement.

Before testing, establish baselines and clarify whether improvement targets represent relative uplift or percentage-point changes.

Next, validate the wireframe with target users and test whether the two-week Circle unlock supports engagement or delays access to useful accountability.
