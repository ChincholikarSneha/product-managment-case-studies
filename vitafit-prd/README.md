#  VitaFit Momentum

## Overview

VitaFit Momentum is a product strategy case study focused on improving user retention in a fitness application. Through user research and product analysis, I identified the reasons behind declining engagement and proposed two features—**Adaptive Coaching Journey** and **Fitness Circles**—to encourage long-term habit formation and improve Day-30 retention. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

---

##  Problem Statement

Users often begin their fitness journey with high motivation but gradually disengage due to repetitive workout experiences, generic reminders, lack of accountability, and limited emotional connection with instructors. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

---

##  Proposed Solution

The solution introduces two complementary engagement features:

- **Adaptive Coaching Journey** – Personalized AI-powered workout plans, coach-style reminders, recovery guidance, and weekly plan adjustments.
- **Fitness Circles** – Small instructor-led communities that promote accountability through shared challenges, milestones, and peer support. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

## Success Metrics

**North Star Metric**

- Day-30 Retention Rate

**Supporting KPIs**

- Increase average weekly workouts per user
- Improve weekly plan completion
- Increase Fitness Circle adoption
- Improve customer satisfaction
- Reduce missed workout rate :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}
