#  Zepto AOV Improvement Strategy

## Overview

This product strategy case study focuses on increasing **Average Order Value (AOV)** by understanding user shopping behaviors and identifying friction points that limit basket size. Through user segmentation, behavioral analysis, and prioritization, I proposed scalable product interventions to encourage users to add more items and explore additional categories. 

---

##  Problem Statement

Most Zepto users are mission-driven and leave the app once their immediate shopping need is fulfilled. Limited product discovery, free-delivery threshold behavior, and trust concerns reduce cross-category purchases and prevent users from increasing their basket size. 

---

##  Proposed Solutions

The strategy proposes five product interventions:

- **Progressive Reward Ladder** – Encourage users to continue shopping beyond the free-delivery threshold with milestone-based rewards.
- **Occasion-Based Bundles** – Curated bundles for common shopping occasions to simplify discovery.
- **Trust-Led Category Expansion** – Build confidence through quality badges, freshness guarantees, and easy replacements.
- **Magic Minute** – Personalized product recommendations based on purchase history.
- **One-Tap Pairs** – Allow users to quickly add forgotten items during checkout. 

---

##  Prioritized Solution

**Progressive Reward Ladder**

Using the **RICE prioritization framework**, the Progressive Reward Ladder was selected as the highest-impact initiative because it addresses a key user behavior, is applicable across the entire user base, and can deliver measurable improvements in AOV with relatively low implementation effort.
