# Zepto AOV Improvement Strategy

## Overview

A product strategy case study exploring how Zepto can increase **Average Order Value (AOV)** by helping customers complete their grocery needs without pressuring them to buy unnecessary items.

The proposed strategy combines an **AI Grocery Planner** with **Flex Credits**, supported by user research, prioritization, a prototype, and an experiment plan.

---

## Problem Statement

Customers often use Zepto for immediate grocery needs and leave after finding their intended items. Forgotten products, discovery friction, price concerns, and uncertainty about availability can limit basket completion.

**Goal:** Increase AOV from the case brief’s baseline of **₹388 to ₹480**, while maintaining order frequency and improving contribution after variable costs.

This is a proposed target, not an achieved result.

---

## Proposed Solutions

### 1. AI Grocery Planner

An optional voice- or text-based planner that turns a customer’s grocery request into an editable shopping list.

- Suggests products based on the stated need, budget, and available catalogue.
- Supports immediate tasks and optional weekly planning.
- Requires customer approval before adding selected products to the cart.
- Uses purchase history only with permission.
- Preserves normal search and checkout.

### 2. Zepto Flex Credits

A delivery-credit concept supporting future urgent grocery orders.

- Earn **₹30 in delivery credit** after delivery of an order containing at least two item units, including identical units.
- Earn up to **two credits per calendar month**.
- Redeem one credit on a later grocery order, including a single-item order, without a minimum basket value.
- Credits expire **30 days after issuance**.
- Separate handling, small-cart, and surge fees remain outside the credit.

---

## Prioritization

Used the **RICE framework** to compare proposed interventions.

Flex Credits ranked highest in the estimated scores, but the **AI Grocery Planner** was selected as the primary basket-growth lever because it directly helps customers discover and approve additional products they need.

Flex Credits support future-order convenience; their direct effect on AOV remains a hypothesis to test.

## Experiment Plan

Begin with a controlled pilot in selected Tier 1 service areas.

Compare four groups:

- Existing experience
- AI Planner only
- Flex Credits only
- AI Planner + Flex Credits

Evaluate all assigned customers to assess AOV, order frequency, retention, and contribution. Expand only if basket growth does not weaken customer or business outcomes.
